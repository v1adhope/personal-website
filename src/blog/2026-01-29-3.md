Thanks to Jon Gjengset for suggesting "The Pragmatic Programmer" by Andy Hunt and Dave Thomas

This book is an engineer’s bible. It reminds you of important practices you learned a long time ago, even if you’re a battle-hardened developer. I wish every beginner would read this first, instead of Robert’s books, which are often far from the real world
