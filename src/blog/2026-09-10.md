I've used Arch Linux for years, and there's exactly one part of it that still punches me in the gut every time: DNS resolution.

Here's what gets me:

- Half a dozen things think they own `/etc/resolv.conf`: `systemd-resolved`, `NetworkManager`, `openresolv`, you name it. The only "standard" is that everything eventually writes to that one file. Then something changes it behind your back anyway, even if you're stacking configs to keep them all in line, and you're left asking, "who the f\*\*ck touched my file?"

- Apps that hijack `/etc/resolv.conf` and never bother restoring it when they're done.

My fix: write the file once, then `chattr +i` so it's immutable and nothing can rewrite it. That's blunt, and I accept all consequences.
