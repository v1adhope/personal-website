---
title: "Choose team processes carefully"
pubDate: 2025-12-21
description: "Observe a team’s needs and remove processes that do not work instead of copying ideas blindly."
tags: ["business", "management"]
---

Some things don't take root in teams at all. They shouldn't be threatened and should be picked carefully. A team is not generic with some input and output values. It's more complex internally. Different mentality, business model, value proposition, key activities, key resources, customer segments, etc. Ask and observe personally if you wanna know their needs. No one wants to spend time on robots or soulless questionnaires. Don't mindlessly copy ideas you've seen from someone else - that's like an iceberg. Stop and eliminate processes that aren't working
