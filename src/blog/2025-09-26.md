title: "Why I chose macOS and Android"
pubDate: 2025-09-26
description: "My experience with Linux, macOS, Windows, iPadOS, and Android."

---

Managing all platforms at once is a challenge. In the end, I picked macOS and Android for my needs.

- Linux is a toy. Every engineer should play with it. New tech, flexibility, configs, and low-level skills. The place where services run.

- macOS (MacBook) is a portable workhorse. A trackpad that feels like an extension of your arm, a Unix-based operating system, a long-lasting battery, and no fans (quiet). Not ideal, but irreplaceable.

- Windows is a human mistake and should die, no discussion. Suitable only for games because of Microsoft's monopoly.

- iPadOS feels like paying for air. A closed system as dumb as a log. A trash new design with a ton of bugs. The beta has fewer than the release. I don't see why it's so highly praised.

- Android is open and free. A smooth, beautiful UI that just works. Messages, calls, and quick notes. Deep integration with Google services out of the box.
