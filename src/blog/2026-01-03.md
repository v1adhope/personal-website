I’ve read Zero to Production in Rust: An Introduction to Backend Development in Rust by Luca Palmieri.

The title speaks for itself. The book walks through core language concepts including the basics, the power of strong types, and RAII, as well as limitations and practical tricks.

I understand why people say Go isn’t a good first language. Simplicity has two sides: you often end up reinventing the wheel for many things, and to do that well, you need solid experience.

Rust is a great language from a developer’s perspective, with a strong community. From a business point of view, it’s more challenging due to lower adoption. Writing in Rust is a pleasure. I can see how many things have been designed well, and I agree with those choices.
