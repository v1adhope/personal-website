Why do I think developers should be engineers, not just backend developers?
Don't waste time rewriting, waiting, or debugging. The business loses less money when you don't guess but know how things work.
Define the right contracts for the frontend so you don't waste time rewriting them or waiting for others to be free to correct your design.
Networking isn't always simple. If you don't know how services are deployed, you'll lose time and may not find out why your app doesn't work, especially if the CI pipeline is hidden from you.
Engineers solve problems. We're products too: choose metrics and reject useless ideas to protect your turf.
Keep safety in mind. Otherwise, the service won't survive.

Why do you write in only one language?
Don't get stuck on it. As a Go developer, I love trying other languages. They broaden your perspective and give you new ways to write the same things. Sometimes that helps make the code clearer.
