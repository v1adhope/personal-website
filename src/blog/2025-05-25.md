Why do I think developers should be engineers not just back end?
Don't waste time for re-write, waiting, or debugging. Business loses less money. You don't guess, you know how it works.
Expose the right contracts for front end and don't waste time re-writing or waiting when others will be free to correct your design.
The network's always not simple if you don't know how services deploying you'll lose time and didn't find a reason why your app doesn't work, because CI might be hidden from you.
Engineers solve problems. We're products too, choose metrics and reject useless ideas to protect the lawn.
Keep safety in mind otherwise the service won't survive.

Why do you write only one language?
Don't be addicted. As a Go developer, I love to try other languages. They expose your mind and give you new ways to write the same thing that you do. Sometimes that helps and makes code clear.
