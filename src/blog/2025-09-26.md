Managing all platforms at once is a challenge. I picked macOS and Android for my needs at the end

- Linux is a toy. Every engineer should play with it. New techs, flexibility, configs, and low-level skills. The place where services revolve

- macOS (MacBook) is a portable working horse. Touchpad as an extension of the arm, a Unix-based operating system, a long-lasting battery, and without fans (quiet). Not ideal, but irreplaceable

- Windows is a human mistake and should die, no discussions. Suitable only for games because of Microsoft's monopoly

- iPadOS pay for air. Closed and stupid system as a log. New trash design with a ton of bugs, beta has fewer than the release. I didn't see why it is so highly praised

- Android is open and free. Smooth and beautiful UI which just works. Messages, calls, and some fast notes. Deep integration with Google services from the box
