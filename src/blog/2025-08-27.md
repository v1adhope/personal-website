The Road to React by Robin Wieruch - clean practice to start your road. Answers on why it works like that. Don't read the first books about regular expressions or manipulation with exotic objects. It's gonna be hell as C++

Object Thinking by David West - a more philosophical book extends your knowledge about true object-oriented programming. Not stupid SOLID (borscht, potatoes, cucumber) or something like that

Concurrency in Go. Tools and Techniques for Developers by Katherine Cox-Buday - valued rules, patterns, constructions on how to write concurrency code, and don't shoot yourself in the foot

Artificial Intelligence Basics. A non-Technical Introduction by Tom Taulli - a good base about machine learning and AI
