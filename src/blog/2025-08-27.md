The Road to React by Robin Wieruch — a practical way to start your journey. It explains why things work the way they do. Don't start with books about regular expressions or manipulating exotic objects. It'll be hell, like C++.

Object Thinking by David West — a more philosophical book that expands your knowledge of true object-oriented programming. Not stupid SOLID (borscht, potatoes, cucumber) or something like that.

Concurrency in Go: Tools and Techniques for Developers by Katherine Cox-Buday — valuable rules, patterns, and constructs for writing concurrent code without shooting yourself in the foot.

Artificial Intelligence Basics: A Non-Technical Introduction by Tom Taulli — a good foundation in machine learning and AI.
