I haven't read a huge number of books in my life. Still, I've noticed a pattern: the more you read, the more books start repeating each other, or their ideas go stale once you've already formed your own opinion. Reading a book the old-fashioned way, cover to cover, takes a lot of time and often isn't worth it.\*

Notebook tools solve this problem for me. I use Gemini Notebook, and there are open-source alternatives too, like Open Notebook.

My usual flow:

- Generate the overview video and watch it.
- Generate a mind map and walk through it section by section.
- Dig into the gaps and skip unnecessary info and fluff.
- Write my own notes.

What I'm still figuring out is how to plug this into my Obsidian knowledge base, so the notes don't just pile up but actually grow it.

\*Nonfiction books only.
