---
title: "Choose team processes carefully"
pubDate: 2025-12-21
description: "Observe a team’s needs and remove processes that do not work instead of copying ideas blindly."
tags: ["business", "management"]
---

Some things don't take root in teams at all. Processes should be chosen carefully. A team isn't a generic function with input and output values. It's more complex internally: different mindsets, business models, value propositions, key activities, key resources, customer segments, etc. Ask people directly and observe if you want to understand their needs. No one wants to spend time on robots or soulless questionnaires. Don't mindlessly copy ideas you've seen someone else use. You're only seeing the tip of the iceberg. Stop and eliminate processes that aren't working.
