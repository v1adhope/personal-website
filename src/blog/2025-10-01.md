How many platforms do you know that you would like to use, but that think differently?

- Hatzner. I had to migrate a VPS from another provider. Went through the complete registration procedure. Enter common data like name, email, etc. (accepted). Enter my foreign credit card (accepted). Provided my passport data and had to wait for approval for my account from their staff to start using the service, but the next day I received a letter which doesn't say anything, just "We think you are suspicious and have removed your account". I can guess that was my Russian passport. So I've left my money Hostinger and am happy
- Telegram. I created a second account to split my life and work. I got the title "Your account is frozen" after few minutes. Tried to send an appellation several times and got ignored
- Twilio. When I started my career as an engineer, I did a project related to emails, and I also got banned without any particular reason
