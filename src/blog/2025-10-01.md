How many platforms do you know that you'd like to use, but that don't want you as a user?

- Hetzner. I needed to migrate a VPS from another provider. I went through the full registration process. I entered basic information like my name and email (accepted). I entered my foreign credit card (accepted). I provided my passport details and had to wait for their staff to approve my account before I could use the service. The next day, I received an email that explained nothing, just "We think you are suspicious and have removed your account". I can only guess it was because of my Russian passport. So I've kept my money with Hostinger and am happy.
- Telegram. I created a second account to separate my personal life from work. After a few minutes, I got the message "Your account is frozen". I tried to submit an appeal several times and was ignored.
- Twilio. When I started my career as an engineer, I worked on an email-related project and also got banned without any particular reason.
