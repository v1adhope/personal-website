Google became a gateway for me to find human reviews, open sites directly by the first link (documentation), and clarify something really quickly. Generally, I use gpt or grok, but they often give a shit. Artificial intelligence works well with generation mocks (data by pattern), concepts, and keywords, extending ideas that I can use and work around. It can't be a teacher of something "professional", books and experience are still valued. AI is just another tool if you don't know what you are searching for, you are lost for today. Waste of time is happening when I try to search for something rare about hardware and low-level, for example
