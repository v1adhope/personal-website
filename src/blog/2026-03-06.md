The Bitcoin Standard by Saifedean Ammous tells about the history of the medium of exchange, the Austrian school compared to others, and e-gold

The era of government-controlled money sucks. The world built a system on property that, as each empire began to fall (Rome, Byzantine), devalued its currency. The government is printing as much money as it wants, with no limits, allowing it to avoid the reality of costs. The central bank's meddling in the capital market is the root of recessions, unemployment, and all the crises that most politicians, journalists, academics, and leftist activists like to blame on capitalism. The economy operates on consumption cycles, and extending national debt encourages individuals to spend their money instead of accumulating capital and adopting a low time preference for the future. The average savings rate of the seven largest advanced economies was 12.66% in 1970, but has dropped to 3.39% in 2015, a fall of almost three-quarters

According to the Austrian view, if the money supply is fixed, then economic growth will cause prices of real goods and services to drop. Scarcity is only relative in material resources, with the differences in cost of extraction being the determinant of the level of scarcity. In fact, the only thing for which the term resource actually applies is human time

Nations using sound money are far more likely to stay peaceful, or to have limited conflict with one another, because sound money places real constraints on the ability of government to finance its military operations

In a productive firm offering valuable service to society, success depends on pleasing customers. Workers are rewarded for how well they do that essential task, and bosses who mistreat their workers will either lose the workers to competitors or destroy their business quickly. In an unproductive firm that does not serve society and relies on bureaucratic largesse for its survival, there is no meaningful standard by which to reward or punish workers

With a sound monetary economy, balance is restored naturally

Gold is a first sound money due to its salability across scales, across space, and time. The Gold Standard system that existed for a thousand years was replaced by bored monarchs during World War

Bitcoin was primarily to avoid centralized control and solve a problem that gold can't. Bitcoin represents the first truly digital solution to the problem of money, and a potential solution to the problems of salability, soundness, and sovereignty, providing the desirable features of physical cash (lack of intermediaries, finality of transactions). Also, it is the first example of absolute scarcity

Blockchain is a technology that eliminates the need for a trusted third party
