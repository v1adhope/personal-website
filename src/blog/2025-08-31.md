---
title: "AI is another tool, not a professional teacher"
pubDate: 2025-08-31
description: "My experience using search engines and AI tools while still valuing books and practical experience."
tags: ["ai"]
---

Google has become a gateway for me to find human reviews, open sites directly from the first result (usually documentation), and clarify things quickly. Generally, I use GPT or Grok, but they often produce shit. Artificial intelligence works well for generating mock data from patterns, exploring concepts and keywords, and expanding ideas I can work with. It can't teach you something at a professional level. Books and experience still matter. AI is just another tool. If you don't know what you're searching for, you're lost for the day. I waste time when I try to search for something obscure about hardware or low-level systems, for example.
