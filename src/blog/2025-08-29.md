In my opinion, you should always extend business to an international level and build things around this, because the local model is weak

- The country's government is changing, and a particular area has become unpredictable. As an option, just leave if something came up or you don't like the rules
- A large variety of tools
- Mixed culture experiences and best practices
- Full market coverage

All said above doesn't mean you should not start a business in your market, which you know well
