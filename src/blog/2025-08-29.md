---
title: "Building a Business for an International Market"
pubDate: 2025-08-29
description: "My perspective on international business, market coverage, tools, and learning from different cultures."
tags: ["business"]
---

In my opinion, you should always expand your business internationally and build with that in mind, because relying only on the local market is fragile.

- Governments change, and a particular market can become unpredictable. Operating internationally gives you the option to leave if something comes up or you don't like the rules.
- A wider variety of tools.
- Experience with different cultures and their best practices.
- Broader market coverage.

None of this means you shouldn't start a business in your own market, which you know well.
